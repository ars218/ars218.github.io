## Apps

### myDecidr
> Journal your decisions. Understand yourself better.

myDecidr helps you record the reasoning behind your choices — capturing your confidence, expected outcome, and logic. Come back later to review how things turned out. Over time, patterns emerge and you understand your own thinking better.

- 📁 [GitHub Repo](https://github.com/ars218/mydecidr)
- 📥 Download APK from the site

### Gradix
> Know your grades. Understand your performance.

Gradix is a grade calculator and performance tracker. Enter your marks per subject and Gradix instantly calculates your percentage, grade, and overall result — with beautiful charts so you always know where you stand.

- 📁 [GitHub Repo](https://github.com/ars218/gradix)
- 📥 Download APK from the site
